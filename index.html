How the company is organized

An import-export company, with a range beyond one factory.

Haiyan Dingtour Hardware Co., Ltd is an import-export company. Under the brand DINGTOUR HARDWARE, it supplies a full range of industrial fasteners to OEM and distributors.

The company has a small in-house factory and works with 30+ partner factories. Supply is not limited to its own factory’s products.

With more than 20 years of industry experience, a catalog of 3,000+ product specifications, and exports to 30+ countries, Dingtour serves overseas buyers as a fastener export partner.
