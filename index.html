How the company is organized

An import-export company, with a range beyond one factory.

Haiyan Dingtour Hardware Co., Ltd is an import-export company. Under the brand DINGTOUR HARDWARE, it supplies a full range of industrial fasteners to OEM and distributors.

The company has a small in-house factory and works with 30+ partner factories. Supply is not limited to its own factory’s products.

With more than 20 years of industry experience, a catalog of 3,000+ product specifications, and exports to 30+ countries, Dingtour serves overseas buyers as a fastener export partner.

Import-export

A China-based export company supplying a full range of industrial fasteners, not a single-plant catalogue.

Own factory, modest scale

A small in-house factory, alongside 30+ partner factories. The export range is not limited to own production.

How to enquire

When you write, please include

Send a written enquiry to the address in the contact section. A complete specification helps us respond accurately.

Enquiry checklist

Use the same points in an email to john.dingtour@gmail.com.

Please include What to specify
Product type The product family or part name — for example bolts, nuts, or a drawing-based part.
Standard / specification The standard you are buying to, or a technical drawing for non-standard parts.
Size Diameter, length, and any other dimensions required for the part.
Material The material specified for the assembly.
Coating The coating or finish specified for the part, if any.
Quantity The quantity required.
Destination The port or region of delivery.
